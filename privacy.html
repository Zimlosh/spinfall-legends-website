Privacy Policy

TEST DRAFT · last updated October 27, 2026

This explains what Spinfall Legends LLC keeps about you and why. We keep as little as we can.

What we keep

Your game: progress, items, settings and your player name. Your birth year, to keep younger players safe. Purchases: what you bought and when (your app store handles payment; we never see your card). Chat and reports: messages you send and reports you make, so we can keep chat safe. Device basics: device type, app version and crash reports, to fix bugs.

What we don't

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone, and we don't sell your personal information.

How we use it

To run the game, save your progress, deliver purchases, keep play fair and chat safe, answer support requests, and improve the game.

Who else sees it

Your app store (for purchases), the services that host the game for us (only to run it), and authorities when the law requires. Other players see your name, level, heroes and guild.

Children

Players under 13 play with typed chat turned off (they can send set quick-chat lines), add friends only by friend code, and should ask a grown-up before any purchase; the app store's own purchase approval applies. We don't knowingly collect more from children than the game needs to run. A parent can ask us to delete a child's account at team@spinfalllegends.com.

Your choices

You can delete your account in Settings at any time; that removes your saved progress. You can ask us what we keep, or to correct or delete it, at team@spinfalllegends.com.

Keeping it safe

We protect what we keep with reasonable care and keep it only as long as the game needs it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the game.
