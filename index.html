Every fight is physics

Aim a dash, block a swing, parry at the perfect moment, then let your ult loose. No auto-battle: you're in control.

41 heroes to collect

From a peasant with a loaf of bread to a demon knight and a fallen angel. Every hero has their own ult and passive, levels up to 100, and can become a veteran.

Tap any hero to see their ult, passive and veteran numbers

Common Rare Legendary

84 weapons to master

Swords, bows, flails, staffs, shields, a war drum, a magic tome... Every one spins differently, and many hide a trick: bursts, poison, worms under the sand.

Tap any weapon to see its ability, numbers and red stars

A journey through 10 lands

Each land has its own three dungeons, a delve that goes on forever, and a world boss at the end.

85 bosses waiting

From mine foremen to the Demon King himself. Some you face alone, some you bring a party for, and some take your whole guild.

11 pets at your side

A pet fights beside you with its own weapon and its own trick: Berry the bear pushes enemies back, the Baby Dragon sets them on fire, the Hellpup bites with all three heads.

Tap any pet to see what it does

The Wanted

Every week a new outlaw roams the dungeons, fighting everyone in its way, you and the monsters alike. Hunt it down for a piece of its legendary weapon.

The Brew Station

Gather 40 ingredients from the enemies of every land, then brew 18 potions at one, two or three stars. Pour two into one bottle for a mix with both effects.

Tap any potion to see it at 1, 2 and 3 stars

The Memory Rift

Step through the Rift into the Memory Trials: maps from every land you've reached, one after another, with remembered enemies that fight dirty. At the end, the Time Capsule holds a relic.

20 relics to wear

One relic at a time, working in every fight: faster spins, harder hits, life steal, quicker ults. Level them up, then give them red stars.

Tap any relic to see what it does at each level

Better together

  • Party up with up to three friends for dungeons and world boss raids.
  • Join a guild: weekly guild bosses, a guild shop, chat and a leaderboard.
  • The Coliseum: 1v1 and 2v2 PvP, first to three rounds. Climb six leagues.
  • No casino: no spinning reels and no paid random rolls. You beat the boss, then you open your chest.
